How we check stories

Check original sources, dates and context before publication. Treat sensitive claims with extra caution; if a claim cannot be verified, do not present it as fact.

Credit and context

Credit original sources and creators, add context in our own words, and link to the original where possible.

Corrections and requests

Found an error? Contact the team through the official Discord to request a correction or takedown. No response deadline is promised.
