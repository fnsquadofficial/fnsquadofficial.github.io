# FN Squad — Public Transparency and Privacy Governance Statement

**Version:** 1.0  
**Published:** 13 November 2026  
**Owner:** FN Squad (independent Malaysia-based community)  
**Official site:** https://fnsquadofficial.github.io/  
**Privacy notice:** https://fnsquadofficial.github.io/privacy/

> **Scope and assurance:** This is a public-facing statement of FN Squad's privacy principles, current website facts, and governance commitments. It is not a certification, statutory registration, regulator approval, or assertion of universal compliance. Compliance depends on applicable law and demonstrated operations. FN Squad's full internal operations handbook is not public, to protect staff and members.

## 1. Who this statement covers

FN Squad operates a public community website and links to WhatsApp, Discord, Minecraft community activities, FreakyNews and social platforms. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. External services are operated by their respective providers and subject to their own terms and notices.

The website is informational. On the 13 November 2026 source review, its own code had **no account registration or evidence upload form, analytics scripts, advertising pixels or embedded social-media widgets**. Public site search/category filtering run in the visitor's browser; FN Squad's code does not transmit the entered search terms to FN Squad.

The site's **fn-theme** value records an optional light/dark appearance preference in browser local storage. The visitor can clear it through browser settings. GitHub Pages separately collects technical hosting information, including IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy policies. Browsing must not be treated as anonymous.

## 2. Our privacy principles

We aim to minimise collection, explain purposes, limit access, protect records from unauthorised use, minimise retention, respond to requests, and consider privacy before adding functionality. These are organisational goals and controls requiring ongoing verification, not a guarantee that every control has already been technically implemented.

For moderation or community reports, administrators may receive usernames, platform-visible contact details, screenshots, timestamps, messages, technical logs and case details. People should redact unrelated identities and avoid sharing passwords, identification documents, home addresses, and other sensitive information in public spaces. Reports may involve third parties; a person's voluntary submission does not automatically authorise unlimited processing of other people's data.

## 3. External platforms, disclosures and transfers

Selecting a WhatsApp, Discord, social-media, gaming or other external link can expose information to that provider according to its privacy practices. A WhatsApp **group** can expose a phone number or profile details to other participants depending on current feature settings; a channel operates differently. Leaving a group cannot guarantee deletion of copies already made.

FN Squad does not sell personal data. It may need to disclose limited data to relevant platform operators, appropriate service providers or authorities where lawful and necessary. Such disclosures must be assessed and restricted. Services may process data outside Malaysia; international transfer requirements vary by jurisdiction and cannot be satisfied merely by linking to a provider policy.

## 4. Rights, private contact and complaints

People may ask about data held by FN Squad, correct inaccurate information, request deletion and enquire about retention and disclosures. Depending on the law, additional rights can include objection, restriction, data portability and consent withdrawal. A request may be limited where evidence must be retained for lawful safety, ongoing investigation or legal reasons.

**Current contact route:** privately message an FN Squad Admin or Moderator through the official Discord community: https://discord.com/invite/ECAwrJNmGm and request the person responsible for privacy. Do not post private evidence to public chats, GitHub issues or social comments.

**Important open item:** A dedicated privacy email address and publicly identified accountable operator have not yet been confirmed. FN Squad must publish these before claiming this governance item is complete.

Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Commissioner / JPDP: https://www.pdp.gov.my/  
Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission complaints portal: https://aduan.mcmc.gov.my/  
Other competent authorities may apply based on the jurisdiction.

## 5. Data retention and security

We seek to limit data access to authorised staff, safeguard accounts, and investigate reported disclosures. The exact implementation, backup deletion and storage of private community reports **have not been independently verified**. Internal proposed retention limits must not be read as automated deletion promises. Requests to delete records are reviewed with due regard to applicable safety, evidence and legal obligations.

Security incidents should be escalated promptly. Applicable breach assessment and notification thresholds and deadlines depend on the relevant law and incident. No single worldwide deadline is asserted.

## 6. Jurisdictional review — not a worldwide compliance certificate

As a Malaysia-based community, FN Squad considers Malaysian **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709)**, the **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024** and applicable JPDP notices and guidelines when its activities fall within scope. Malaysian **MCMC** rules cover different communications and multimedia matters and are not a data-protection certification.

International screening references include:

## 7. ISO and accessibility benchmarks

Internal privacy-security planning draws on **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**, **ISO/IEC 27002:2022**, **ISO/IEC 27701:2025**, privacy impact-assessment and risk-management guidance, and **WCAG 2.2 AA** as a website accessibility target. These are standards or benchmarks; FN Squad **does not claim ISO certification, conformity assessment, regulator endorsement or complete global legal compliance**.

## 8. Transparency, updates and accountability

The public website privacy notice is the first point of reference. This statement provides additional governance context; where an actual site feature or third-party provider differs, the notice must be corrected and users given accurate information. Before launching accounts, forms, analytics, payments or non-essential tracking, assess and document processing, legal bases, notices, consents, vendors, retention and safeguards.

This statement will be reviewed when practices or laws materially change. The public may submit concerns through the private route above. Nothing in it diminishes rights granted by applicable law.
