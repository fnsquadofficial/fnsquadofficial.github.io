SAFETY & MODERATION

Need to report something?

For urgent community moderation, contact an FN Squad Admin or Moderator privately on our official Discord. For a written enquiry, you can also use fnsquadofficial@gmail.com.

Include relevant dates, links and usernames, but remove unnecessary personal information from evidence. Do not post IDs, passwords, home addresses or private screenshots in public channels. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying on FN Squad.

We do not promise a fixed response time. Platform-specific issues may need to be raised with the platform provider.

BAHASA MELAYU

Hubungi FN Squad

Alamat e-mel rasmi FN Squad ialah fnsquadofficial@gmail.com. Sila gunakan alamat ini bagi pertanyaan umum, cadangan kerjasama, urusan pemasaran, permohonan pembetulan kandungan, isu hak cipta serta pertanyaan berkaitan privasi dan data peribadi.
