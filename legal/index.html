01. Legal notices and community policies

FN Squad publishes practical, accessible policies for its public website and community. The documents below serve different purposes and should be read together where relevant.

Privacy Notice / Notis PrivasiWebsite storage, personal information, reporting and rights. Terms of Use / Terma PenggunaanAcceptable use, copyright, third-party services and limitations. Community Rules / Peraturan KomunitiConduct, safeguarding, reports and moderation. Editorial Standards / Piawaian EditorialSources, corrections, opinion and public-interest reporting. Transparency / KetelusanPublic governance statement and source-based legal screening.

Copyright and disclaimer provisions are included in the Terms of Use. Website storage is explained in the Privacy Notice; no separate cookie-consent banner is introduced without a reason established by the actual technologies used.

02. Contact, complaints and rights

For general or privacy-related enquiries, email the official inbox at fnsquadofficial@gmail.com, or contact an FN Squad administrator or moderator privately through the official Discord community. See our Contact page for enquiry options. Do not publish sensitive personal information in public channels.

Outstanding governance items: the team's four public leadership roles are identified, and the official email inbox is published. However, a formally designated privacy contact person, verified request-handling procedures and a documented, implemented retention schedule still need confirmation. We do not claim the community is an incorporated company or that a Data Protection Officer has been formally appointed.

03. Accessibility

FN Squad aims to make its website usable with smaller screens, keyboard navigation and assistive technologies. The W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 are an appropriate technical benchmark. No complete accessibility audit or WCAG Level AA conformance claim is made here.

Report a broken link, inaccessible control or difficult-to-read content through the contact route above and identify the page and device or browser where possible.

04. Malaysian and international reference points

For language, this site refers to Perkara 152 Perlembagaan Persekutuan and Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka guidance. For privacy, it refers to Malaysia's Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi and the laws listed in the Privacy Notice's jurisdiction table. References are not certifications or government endorsements.

Pusat Dasar dan Terma FN Squad

FN Squad menyediakan notis dan panduan awam untuk menjelaskan cara laman web dan komunitinya dikendalikan. Notis Privasi menerangkan maklumat peribadi serta hak pengguna; Terma dan Syarat Penggunaan menerangkan penggunaan laman web dan hak cipta; Peraturan Komuniti menerangkan tatakelakuan dan tindakan penguatkuasaan; manakala Piawaian Editorial menerangkan kaedah penilaian sumber dan pembetulan kandungan.

Pertanyaan dan aduan: E-mel rasmi FN Squad ialah fnsquadofficial@gmail.com. Anda juga boleh menghubungi pentadbir atau moderator secara peribadi melalui Discord rasmi. Elakkan menyiarkan dokumen dan maklumat peribadi dalam saluran awam. Pasukan kepimpinan empat orang dinyatakan pada halaman Pengenalan, namun pegawai yang ditetapkan secara rasmi bagi urusan privasi serta tatacara penyimpanan rekod masih memerlukan pengesahan.

Kebolehcapaian: FN Squad berusaha membangunkan laman web yang boleh digunakan melalui pelbagai peranti dan kaedah navigasi. Garis Panduan Kebolehcapaian Kandungan Web (WCAG) 2.2 dijadikan rujukan teknikal, namun pematuhan pada tahap tertentu belum dibuktikan melalui audit menyeluruh.

Penggunaan bahasa Melayu berpandukan kedudukannya sebagai bahasa kebangsaan menurut Perkara 152 Perlembagaan Persekutuan serta pedoman DBP. FN Squad bukan agensi kerajaan, dan rujukan kepada undang-undang, agensi atau piawaian tidak bermaksud mendapat pengesahan rasmi.
