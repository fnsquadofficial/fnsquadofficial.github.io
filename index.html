GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

What is FN Squad?

FN Squad is the umbrella community connecting FreakyNews, the news-and-memes channel, and FN SMP, the Minecraft community.

Where can I follow FreakyNews?

The official WhatsApp channel link needs to be added by the site owner before this website can send you there. Use the connection button below once it has been configured.

How do I join FN SMP?

The official server address, edition, version, and rules should be published once confirmed. The site intentionally does not guess these details.

What are the FN SMP rules?

1. No hacks, x-ray or exploits. Report bugs! 2. No griefing or stealing, even if unlocked. 3. PvP only with consent. 4. Respect claims and build 100+ blocks from others. 5. No offensive builds, signs or chat. 6. One main account; declare alts. 7. Staff decisions follow our strike system, and you can appeal in Discord within 14 days.

How are FreakyNews posts labelled?

Posts are marked Confirmed, Reported, Rumour or Opinion, with a source and date. We verify before we post, credit creators and correct mistakes quickly.

Does the feed show live news?

Not yet. The cards above demonstrate the site's content layout. A verified content source or manual publishing workflow must be connected before they can represent current stories.
