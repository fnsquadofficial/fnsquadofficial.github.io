GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

What is FN Squad?

FN Squad is the umbrella community connecting FreakyNews, our news-and-memes WhatsApp channel, and FN SMP, our Minecraft survival community. Our values: fun first, respect always, truth matters, and fair for everyone.

Where can I follow FreakyNews?

Use the Follow FreakyNews button below to open the official WhatsApp channel. The channel is one-way, so join the linked discussion group to chat.

How do I join FN SMP?

Join the FN Squad Discord and apply in #whitelist-apply. Full steps and the server rules are on the FN SMP page.

What are the community rules?

Respect everyone, keep it safe and clean, and report problems to any Admin or Mod. Read the Community page for the full rules, how reporting works and what happens after a rule break.

What does FreakyNews cover?

Motorsports and athletics (F1, MotoGP, global football), global affairs, entertainment and media (films, streaming, anime, pop culture), and gaming and hobbies. Content shared is for informational purposes only. We do not intend to promote, endorse or show bias toward any individuals, organizations or ideologies. See how we label and verify posts.

Does the feed show live news?

Not yet. The cards on this site demonstrate the layout and are marked as content slots. Real posts are published on the WhatsApp channel.
