FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The useful stuff.

Joining & connecting

Which WhatsApp group is FN Squad's main group?

FreakyHomie is FN Squad's official main WhatsApp group. Open the official invitation. WhatsApp group members may see your phone number, depending on your platform settings.

Where are the official news and announcements channels?

Follow FreakyNews on WhatsApp for channel posts and FN Squad Updates for community announcements. Groups are for discussion; channels are for published updates.

How do I join the Discord community?

Use the official FN Squad Discord invite. Follow Discord's applicable age requirements and our community rules.

How do I know a link is official?

Start at the FN Squad official links directory. If someone sends a suspicious link or asks for a password, check with a moderator before opening it. Staff will never ask you for account passwords.

FN SMP / Minecraft

How can I join FN SMP?

Read the FN SMP page, join our Discord, and check the current whitelist application instructions. You can also join the FN SMP WhatsApp group for community discussion.

What is the Minecraft server address and version?

Server connection details are published on FN SMP server details when the team confirms them. We don't show guessed IP addresses, versions or online status.

Can I grief, steal or use hacked clients?

No. FN SMP has rules against griefing, theft, cheating and harassment. Read the server rules before playing.

FreakyNews / content

Is the website's FreakyNews feed live?

The site may contain preview content rather than live reporting. Check the FreakyNews page and official WhatsApp channel for currently published material. Preview blocks are not real news stories.

How do I request a correction or report a copyright issue?

Write to fnsquadofficial@gmail.com and include the link, the issue and a supporting source where possible. See our Editorial Standards. We cannot promise a particular response deadline.

Reports, privacy & safety

How do I report bullying, scams or unsafe behaviour?

Contact an Admin or Moderator privately through Discord or use fnsquadofficial@gmail.com. Include only relevant evidence. Do not post other people's sensitive information in public channels. Read the rules and reporting privacy notice.

Can I appeal a moderation decision?

Refer to the Community Rules and appeals process. The published FN Squad process describes appeals within 14 days, where applicable. Ask an administrator for the current channel and any case reference.

How do I ask FN Squad to correct or delete my personal information?

Email fnsquadofficial@gmail.com or see the Privacy Notice. Mention the platform, username and approximate date. Do not send ID documents unless the team explains why verification is required.

How do I change the website theme?

Use the light/dark theme button in the website header. Your choice is saved locally in this browser under fn-theme; it can be cleared through your browser's site-data settings.

BAHASA MELAYU

Pusat Bantuan FN Squad

Untuk menyertai komuniti, gunakan pautan rasmi pada direktori FN Squad. Kumpulan WhatsApp utama ialah FreakyHomie. Bagi permainan Minecraft, sila rujuk halaman FN SMP untuk syarat penyertaan, peraturan serta maklumat pelayan yang telah disahkan.

Aduan berhubung salah laku hendaklah disampaikan secara peribadi kepada pentadbir atau moderator melalui Discord rasmi, atau melalui fnsquadofficial@gmail.com. Elakkan penyiaran maklumat peribadi dan bahan bukti sensitif dalam ruang awam. Permohonan pembetulan kandungan serta pertanyaan mengenai data peribadi juga boleh dikemukakan melalui alamat e-mel rasmi.

Untuk maklumat lanjut, baca Peraturan Komuniti, Notis Privasi dan Terma dan Syarat Penggunaan. Tiada tempoh maklum balas tertentu yang dijamin.
