Truth matters

FreakyNews posts what we can stand behind.

Fair for everyone

The same rules for staff, friends and newcomers.

Community rules

Breaking rules means a warning, a mute, then removal. Report issues to any Admin or Mod.

  1. Respect everyone. No hate, bullying or doxxing.
  2. No race, religion or royalty (3R) attacks. Malaysia is multiracial and multi-religious.
  3. No spam, scams, gambling, 18+ content or gore.
  4. No unverified rumours or private screenshots.
  5. No ads or links without Admin approval.
  6. Stay on topic. Be kind, keep it fun.
Friendly roasting

Allowed only between people who both clearly enjoy it. If someone says stop, you stop.

Keeping it safe

No sexual or pornographic content, no gore, no shock content, and nothing that promotes self-harm, violence or illegal activity. Don't share private info (phone numbers, addresses, IC numbers, photos, chat screenshots) without the person's consent. Gambling, betting, loans, adult services and investment schemes are banned, and so are scam links, phishing and fake giveaways.

Platform notes

The FreakyNews WhatsApp channel is one-way, so use the linked discussion group. On Discord, use the right channel, keep nicknames readable and don't impersonate staff. Instagram and TikTok comments follow the same rules, and giveaways need clear rules and Admin sign-off.

Age and safety

Minimum ages

Check Discord’s minimum age for your country before joining. Our WhatsApp directory labels chat groups and announcement channels separately. Follow WhatsApp’s age requirements. Members under 18 get stricter moderation.

Adults and minors

Adults must not DM minors for non-community reasons. Inappropriate messages toward anyone who appears to be a minor mean an instant ban and may be reported.

Protect yourself

Never share personal details publicly, and never meet strangers from the community alone.

How to report

DM any Admin or Mod, or use #how-to-report on Discord. Include screenshots that show the username, message, date and time. Redact unrelated names, messages and personal details before sending evidence. Send evidence privately to an Admin or Mod. Read our reporting privacy notice before submitting. Deliberately false reports count as a violation.

What happens after a rule break

Strike 1

Warning, plus a short mute where relevant.

Strike 2

Temporary suspension of 3 to 14 days.

Strike 3

Permanent removal.

Severe offences

Hate, threats, scams, hacks and anything involving minors mean an instant ban.

Strikes 1 and 2 expire after 90 clean days. You can appeal within 14 days using your case number, and a different Admin reviews it.

Need help?

In immediate danger, call 999.

Befrienders KL: 03-7627 2929 (emotional support). Talian Kasih: 15999 (child welfare concerns).

FN Squad staff are volunteers, not professionals. If someone is in crisis, we'll respond with care and point to these services.

Money and data

No pay-to-win. Ever.

Donations are optional and go to running costs.

Minors and money

We never ask minors for money. Payments from under-18s need a parent or guardian's clear involvement.

Minimal data

We keep your data minimal and private, and never sell or share it. This website uses no analytics or tracking. Ask any Admin what we hold about you.
