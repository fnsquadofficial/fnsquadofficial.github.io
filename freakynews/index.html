How FreakyNews works

Fast, fun and accurate. Speed never beats accuracy. Every published digest entry should identify its source, publication date and reporting context. We do not claim to independently verify every source statement.

Official source

A statement or update attributed to the organisation that issued it; that is not independent verification.

Reported

A sourced report from a news outlet, with the publisher identified.

Context

Our summary explains timing and limitations without inventing claims or extrapolating beyond the source.

Corrections

Errors can be raised with the team; updates should be dated and made transparently.

  1. Use an identifiable original source and state when it published the information.
  2. Credit reporting outlets, companies, creators and official announcements accurately.
  3. Avoid unsourced accusations, personal information and misleading headlines.
  4. Apply extra caution to sensitive public-interest topics, particularly Malaysia’s 3R issues.
  5. Invite correction requests and update or withdraw inaccurate entries when warranted.

Got a story or correction?

Email fnsquadofficial@gmail.com with the original public source link, the publication date and why the story matters. Do not send sensitive personal evidence in a public group. A tip is not treated as fact until checked.

Spotted a mistake? Send the story link and supporting source to the official email or see the Editorial Standards. We will review requests, but cannot guarantee a specific response deadline.
