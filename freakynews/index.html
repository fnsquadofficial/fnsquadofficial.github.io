How FreakyNews works

Fast, fun and accurate. Speed never beats accuracy. Every post carries a label, a source and a date.

Confirmed

Verified with reliable sources.

Reported

A source says it, but we haven't verified it independently.

Rumour

Unverified and clearly labelled as such.

Opinion

Reviews and takes, not facts.

  1. We verify before we post.
  2. We credit sources and creators.
  3. We never post private info, graphic content or hate.
  4. We keep race, religion and royalty topics under extra care, and hold a story when unsure.
  5. We correct mistakes fast and visibly.

Got news? Send a tip

DM an FN Editor with what happened, a link or screenshot as evidence, and when it happened. Tell us if you want credit or want to stay anonymous. Tips are checked before posting, so it may take time.

Spotted a mistake? Anyone can request a correction or takedown by DM. Editors respond within 24 hours.
