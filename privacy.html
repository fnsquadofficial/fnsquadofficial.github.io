FN Squad privacy notice

Website visits

The site stores your theme choice in your browser if you change it. You can remove it by clearing site data. The website does not ask you to sign in or upload evidence. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Discord and WhatsApp apply their own privacy policies when you use their services.

Community reports

If you report an incident privately to a FN Squad Admin or Mod on Discord, your report may include usernames, messages, timestamps and screenshots. Staff use this information to investigate incidents, enforce community rules and review appeals. Minecraft server logs may also be reviewed for FN SMP cases.

Send only relevant evidence and redact unrelated details. Avoid posting evidence in public channels. Before submitting sensitive material, ask staff who may access it, how long they expect to keep it, and when it could be disclosed.

For privacy questions or to request access, correction or deletion of report information, contact a FN Squad Admin or Mod privately through the Discord link on the Community page.

Jurisdiction and standards

FN Squad is based in Malaysia. Malaysian law applies where relevant. Laws in other jurisdictions may also apply depending on the service and how it is used.

Our source review uses ISO/IEC 40500 and WCAG 2.2 for accessibility, ISO/IEC 25010 for software quality, and ISO/IEC 27001 for information security management. We also reviewed the applicability of Malaysian privacy law, ASEAN data governance guidance, and selected EU privacy and accessibility rules.

This review provides no certification or legal compliance determination. The United States, Canada, China and Russia were outside the jurisdiction-specific review.

Notis privasi FN Squad

FN Squad berpangkalan di Malaysia. Undang-undang Malaysia terpakai apabila berkaitan. Undang-undang bidang kuasa lain juga mungkin terpakai bergantung pada perkhidmatan dan cara penggunaannya.

Lawatan ke laman web

Laman ini menyimpan pilihan tema dalam pelayar apabila anda menukarnya. Anda boleh memadam pilihan itu dengan mengosongkan data laman. Laman web ini tidak meminta anda log masuk atau memuat naik bukti. GitHub Pages mengehos laman ini dan merekod alamat IP pelawat untuk tujuan keselamatan. Discord dan WhatsApp menggunakan dasar privasi masing-masing apabila anda menggunakan perkhidmatan mereka.

Laporan komuniti

Jika anda membuat laporan secara peribadi kepada Admin atau Moderator FN Squad di Discord, laporan itu mungkin mengandungi nama pengguna, mesej, tarikh, masa dan tangkapan skrin. Petugas menggunakan maklumat ini untuk menyiasat insiden, menguatkuasakan peraturan komuniti dan menyemak rayuan. Log pelayan Minecraft juga boleh disemak bagi kes FN SMP.

Hantar bukti yang berkaitan sahaja dan padam butiran yang tidak berkaitan. Elakkan menyiarkan bukti di saluran awam. Sebelum menghantar bahan sensitif, tanya petugas siapa yang boleh mengaksesnya, berapa lama bahan itu dijangka disimpan dan bila ia mungkin didedahkan.

Untuk pertanyaan privasi atau permohonan akses, pembetulan atau pemadaman maklumat laporan, hubungi Admin atau Moderator FN Squad secara peribadi melalui pautan Discord di halaman Komuniti.
